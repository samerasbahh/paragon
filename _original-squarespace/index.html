Company Overview

Paragon Engineering is a design-build company specialized in contracting, renovations and finishing works; with a capacity to deliver works from initial conceptual design to turnkey handover. Committed to punctuality, integrity and excellence, the company was established in 2019.

Our Vision

Paragon supports it’s clients with timely, innovative and reliable engineering solutions that are responsive to the needs of each client. Our vision is to promote services provided to a higher level; worthy of our customers in terms of quality, safety, and commitment to time and budget. All our works are guaranteed and warrantied as they are implemented by an experienced technical team, supervised by qualified engineers and performed according to international specifications and standards.
